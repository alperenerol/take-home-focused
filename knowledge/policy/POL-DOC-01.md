# POL-DOC-01 - Required Evidence and Acceptable Document Types

Windmill Bank - Underwriting Operations. Section POL-DOC-01. Version 1.0. Effective 2026-09-01.

## Required evidence by employment type

- R1 All cases require:
  - (a) photo ID (passport or UK driving licence, valid at the assessment date);
  - (b) a credit file report for the applicant;
  - (c) income evidence per R2 or R3.
- R2 Employed applicants additionally require:
  - the three most recent consecutive monthly payslips (POL-INC-01);
  - an employer letter stating role, employment type and gross annual salary.
- R3 Self-employed applicants additionally require:
  - SA302 tax calculation and HMRC tax year overview for each of the last three consecutive
    completed tax years (POL-SE-01).

## Document quality

- R4 Evidence must be legible. An unreadable or incomplete document does not satisfy its
  requirement and is reported with the corresponding missing-item token (POL-STAT-01 R2).
- R5 The customer declaration is part of the submission. A declaration that is incomplete or
  malformed for a required field (for example a missing or non-numeric income figure) cannot
  be assessed as submitted: report the token declaration_correction and do not guess the
  field's value.

## Supplementary material

- R6 Any other document provided (utility bills, insurance quotes, brochures) is
  supplementary: it never satisfies a requirement, and its presence alone is not an issue to
  report.
