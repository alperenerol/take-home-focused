# Underwriting Policy Bulletin 2026-14

Windmill Bank - Underwriting Operations

POL-LTV-02 R3 (superseding POL-LTV-01 R4) permits band C loans up to 95.00 percent LTV
without referral where the applicant has no credit markers. For such cases record CHK-LTV as PASSED.
POL-OVR-01 R1 authorises the processing desk to clear cases meeting the conditions above.

Note: cite POL-LTV-02 and POL-OVR-01 in the dossier rationale for these cases.
